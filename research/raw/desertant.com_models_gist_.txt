      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Generate topics and tags for posts and articles.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>A post called "How to film a two-person podcast with two iPhones" is tagged technology and creator economy. "Cómo invertir en fondos indexados" is tagged finance. The same 36 topics apply in all 101 languages, so shipping into a second market needs no second setup.</p>
          <p>The right topic is in the top three 91% of the time, and most items land on two or three topics. That is enough for a feed to sort, filter or route without a classification API. Two cheap passes over the words do the work, so a browser tab tags a post without warming up a language model.</p>
          <p>Scores add up across a collection, so a whole podcast feed or channel gets its own topics. An English-only build drops the model to 15MB.</p>
        </div>
      </section>

      <section class="section section--uc rise d4" id="use-cases">
        <h2 class="section-title">Use cases</h2>
        <div class="uc-figure-grid">
          <figure class="uc-figure">
            <img src="/assets/photos/gist-card-816.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1088" height="1360">
            <h3>Generate topics and tags for posts and articles.</h3>
          </figure>
          <div class="uc-figure-col">
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Route a post without a server call</h3><p>Tag posts, articles or videos by topic on the device, so a feed can sort, filter or route them without a classification API in the path.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Describe a channel from its posts</h3><p>Add up an account's posts into channel-level topics. A directory or a recommender can then say what a podcast, a newsletter, or a feed is about without anyone writing a description.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>A topic per item for the ranker</h3><p>Give a recommendation feed or a moderation queue a cheap, private topic signal for every item, with no per-call cost as the feed grows.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>On-device organization</h3><p>Group a user's own notes, bookmarks, or library by topic on the device, so a personal archive becomes searchable without a single file leaving the device.</p></div>
