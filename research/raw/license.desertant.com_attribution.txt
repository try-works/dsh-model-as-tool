<h1 id="attribution">Attribution</h1>
<p>Our models and SDKs are free to use in your product for most apps, up to 100,000
monthly active devices per platform. In return, we ask that you credit Desert Ant Labs
somewhere your users can find it. We've kept it easy and flexible.</p>
<p>The formal wording is <a href="/1.0">Section 9 of the license</a>.</p>
<h2 id="what-a-good-credit-looks-like">What a good credit looks like</h2>
<p>A short line that names Desert Ant Labs and links to <a href="https://desertant.com">https://desertant.com</a>:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Powered by <a href="https://desertant.com">Desert Ant Labs</a> 🐜</strong></li>
<li><strong>On-device AI by <a href="https://desertant.com">Desert Ant Labs</a></strong></li>
<li><strong>Emo by <a href="https://desertant.com">Desert Ant Labs</a></strong>: naming the specific model is great too</li>
<li>The ant (🐜) may sit next to the name (<strong>Powered by Desert Ant Labs 🐜</strong>), but it does
  not replace the name.</li>
</ul>
<p>Where the medium can't carry a link (offline use, an embedded surface, an exported
image), the text on its own is fine.</p>
<h3 id="what-doesnt-count">What doesn't count</h3>
<ul>
<li>"Desert Ant Labs" alone, with no context, in a place users won't find.</li>
<li>A credit hidden where a user could never reasonably reach it.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="where-to-put-it">Where to put it</h2>
<p><strong>One placement</strong> in a reasonably discoverable location is enough. You don't have to
show it on every screen or during use. Any of these works:</p>
<ul>
<li>an About, Settings, Credits, Licenses, or Acknowledgements screen</li>
<li>your App Store / Google Play listing</li>
<li>an about, legal, or licenses page on your website</li>
</ul>
<p>For a product with no user-visible surface (a background service, say), crediting us
in your documentation or repository is enough.</p>
<h2 id="logo-option">Logo option</h2>
<p>Instead of, or alongside, the text credit you may use the Desert Ant Labs logo. The
full lockup is preferred; the ant mark on its own is for tight spaces (a favicon, an
app-icon corner).</p>
