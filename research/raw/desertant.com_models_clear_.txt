      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Studio sound, no cloud bill.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>Adobe Podcast, Dolby and Auphonic bill by the minute and require audio or video to be uploaded to their servers. Clear runs a 5-minute clip in 1s on an iPhone 16 Pro, on the device.</p>
          <p>Record on a laptop or phone mic in an untreated room with traffic outside, and the result sounds close-miked: warm low-mids, the voice forward, no background noise. With output presets for Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube loudness.</p>
          <p>Clear is available in two variants: <code>clear-studio</code> and <code>clear-natural</code>. Studio, the default, cleans down to near silence. Natural keeps room tone, breath and lip texture for treated rooms and intentional voiceover.</p>
        </div>
      </section>

Speed on Android and in the browser is not measured yet, and we have not published an audio-quality score against the cloud tools.</p>

        <h2 class="section-title">Use cases</h2>
        <div class="uc-figure-grid">
          <figure class="uc-figure">
            <img src="/assets/screens/clear-usecase.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1088" height="1360">
            <h3>Rescue a recording from a bad room.</h3>

          <div class="uc-figure-col">
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Podcast and video mastering</h3><p>Clean a laptop-mic or untreated-room recording in one pass. Pick Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, or EBU R128, and Clear automatically normalizes the volume to their specification.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Kill the room, not only the hiss</h3><p>Most denoisers stop at the hiss. Clear removes the reflections too, so a podcast, video or meeting recorded at home or in a conference room sounds close-miked instead of boxy.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>No upload, no per-minute bill</h3><p>Studio cleanup as a feature has no metered pricing for users. Enhancement runs on-device, so there is no cloud audio bill that grows with every minute of audio processing.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Fix an hour-long recording on a phone</h3><p>The file path streams chunk by chunk when writing to disk, so a one-hour recording uses the same memory as a ten-second clip. The enhanced file lands next to the original.</p></div>

      <section class="section rise d5">
        <h2 class="section-title">What the model does</h2>
        <ul class="features">
          <li>Trained for a rich, present, close-miked podcast sound: low-mids forward, no thin or distant voice.</li>
          <li>Denoise plus dereverb from a fine-tuned DeepFilterNet 3 model. An untreated bedroom or hotel room comes out closer to a treated studio, with no reverberation added back.</li>
          <li>Sibilance-safe and artifact-free: no harsh peaks on S, T and F, and no pumping or musical noise. Breaths and plosives stay intact.</li>
          <li>Volume presets: Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, EBU R128 (LUFS / EBU R128 normalization). Output is 48kHz.</li>
          <li>Decodes any audio file AVFoundation can read, and the output extension picks the encoding: <code>.wav</code> for 16-bit PCM, <code>.m4a</code>, <code>.mp4</code>, or <code>.aac</code> for AAC, <code>.caf</code> or <code>.aiff</code> for PCM. Without a filesystem, <code>enhance(bytes:)</code> returns WAV bytes.</li>
          <li>Two variants: <code>clear-studio</code> for a quiet studio cleanup, <code>clear-natural</code> to keep room tone. The weights are fetched from the Hub on first use and cached. <code>Strength</code> and <code>Mastering</code> controls on both to adjust to taste.</li>
        </ul>

      <div class="caveats rise d6">
        <p class="model-caveat">Clear is built for speech. Music, effects, and other non-vocal sound get pulled down as noise, so run Clear on a voice track, not a finished mix.</p>
        <p class="model-caveat">Clear is not a source separator either: two people talking over each other stay overlapped. <code>clear-studio</code> cleans hard enough to strip breath and room tone, so reach for <code>clear-natural</code> in a treated room.</p>
