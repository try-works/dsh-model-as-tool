      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Tag and rank images.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>A burst leaves forty near-identical photos and a person who will never sort them. Eye picks the sharp, well-composed one and marks the rest as duplicates.</p>
          <p>The quality answer breaks into five scores: interestingness, composition, subject clarity, thumbnail quality and aesthetic. Sort on the score to find the best photo in a burst or the best thumbnail in a clip. Eye also fingerprints each image, so near-identical shots group without comparing pixels.</p>
        </div>
      </section>

      <section class="section section--uc rise d4" id="use-cases">
        <h2 class="section-title">Use cases</h2>
        <div class="uc-grid"><div class="uc-col">
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Keep one, not forty</h3><p>Score a burst and surface the keeper, so a user sees one good photo instead of scrolling forty near identical ones. Runs on the device, so a camera roll never gets uploaded to be sorted.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Camera roll cleanup</h3><p>Spot near-duplicates across a library and offer to keep the best one, on the phone, so a cleanup feature never uploads a customer's photos.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Pick the cover frame for a video</h3><p>Score candidate frames for composition, subject clarity, and thumbnail quality, and let a video app propose a cover image the creator does not have to hunt for.</p></div>
        </div></div>
      </section>

      <section class="section rise d5">
        <h2 class="section-title">What the model does</h2>
        <ul class="features">
          <li>One call returns a pick score, quality axes (interestingness, composition, thumbnail quality, subject clarity, aesthetic), a scene class, and tags and concepts.</li>
          <li>Perceptual embedding for near-duplicate and burst dedupe.</li>
          <li>Optional <code>EyeFaces</code> module (+22MB), identity-blind by design, with no face recognition.</li>
        </ul>
      </section>
