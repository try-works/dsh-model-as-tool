      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Extract typed JSON from any text.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>Write "Lunch with Priya next Thursday at 1" and the event is typed: the date resolved against the device clock, the duration a number, the title a string. No parsing step, no schema validation, no retry loop.</p>
          <p>Nothing is invented either. Schemer leaves a missing field empty 91% of the time; prompted LLMs manage 18-43%. Extracted strings are verbatim substrings of the input with character offsets, so auditing is a substring check. Schemer works from plain JSON Schema, the shape OpenAI and Gemini accept, in any of 13 languages.</p>
        </div>
      </section>
      <section class="section section--perf rise d4" id="performance" aria-label="Performance">

        <p class="section-lede">Schemer scores 0.800 on schemas it has never seen, at 211M parameters and 230MB. The only models that score higher are 40 times its size or larger.</p>

      <section class="section section--uc rise d4" id="use-cases">
        <h2 class="section-title">Use cases</h2>
        <div class="uc-figure-grid">
          <figure class="uc-figure">
            <img src="/assets/photos/schemer-card-816.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1088" height="1360">
            <h3>Extract typed JSON from any text.</h3>
          </figure>
          <div class="uc-figure-col">
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Natural language to calendar entry</h3><p>Turn "Lunch with Priya next Thursday at 1, about an hour" into a typed event: title, start datetime, duration, and recurrence. Runs in a keyboard or notes app, on device and offline.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Messages into structured records</h3><p>Turn a chat message, email, or scanned note into a CRM lead, order, or invoice. Names, emails, amounts, and dates arrive decoded to their real type, a missing field arrives as null, and the record is ready to save without a parsing step.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>On-device agents and form-fill</h3><p>Give a local agent a JSON Schema and free text, and the typed JSON it gets triggers an action or prefills a form. No cloud LLM, no per-call cost.</p></div>

      <section class="section rise d5">
        <h2 class="section-title">What the model does</h2>
        <ul class="features">
          <li>Works from plain JSON Schema, the one OpenAI and Gemini accept, and writes typed JSON that matches it.</li>
          <li>Decodes strings, numbers, booleans, datetimes, labels, and arrays to their real types.</li>
          <li>Explicit absence detection: reports a field as missing instead of inventing a value.</li>
          <li>Works across 13 languages from a single model, with no per-language setup.</li>
        </ul>
      </section>


      <section class="section rise d5">
        <h2 class="section-title">Specs</h2>
        <dl class="specs">
          <div><dt>Model</dt><dd>211M parameters</dd></div>
          <div><dt>Accuracy</dt><dd>0.800 (int8), 0.911 absence detection</dd></div>
          <div><dt>On-device size</dt><dd>230MB per platform (int8)</dd></div>
          <div><dt>Formats</dt><dd>Core ML and LiteRT, cross-platform</dd></div>
        </dl>
      </section>
      <div class="caveats rise d6">
        <p class="model-caveat">Schemer copies what the text states; Schemer does not write. Ask for an abstractive title or a judgment that needs world knowledge and a much larger model will beat it.</p>
        <p class="model-caveat">Schemer is not an entity extractor either: it fills the fields you declared, and does not enumerate every name in the paragraph. Nested schemas are handled by the harness rather than the model, and quality there is measured on internal templates rather than messy real-world prose.</p>
      </div>
