<h3 id="options">Options</h3>
<p>Every SDK takes the same three options:</p>
<table><thead><tr><th>Option</th><th>Default</th><th></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><code>threshold</code></td><td><code>0.<wbr>5</code></td><td>Score at or above which <code>isNSFW</code> is true. A product dial: raise it to trade recall for precision.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>policy</code></td><td>standard</td><td><code>allowTopless</code> ignores a bare chest on its own; exposed genitals or buttocks, full nudity, and sexual activity still flag.</td></tr>
<tr><td><code>quality</code></td><td>accurate</td><td>Crops scored per image, max taken. <code>fast</code> is one center crop (video frames), <code>balanced</code> four multiscale tiles, <code>accurate</code> those tiles and their mirrors, the setting the model is evaluated with.</td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>The score is the max of the region heads the policy counts. Regions are decision
scores, not calibrated probabilities.</p>
<h3 id="loading-the-model">Loading the model</h3>
<p>The weights are fetched from the Hub on first use and cached. See
<a href="https://github.com/Desert-Ant-Labs/desert-ant-core/blob/v3.5.0/README.md#model-downloads-and-caching">model downloads and caching</a>.</p>
