<p>Older revisions (tags <code>v0.6.0</code> and earlier) carry <code>Emo.mlmodelc</code> and <code>emo.safetensors</code> for SDK versions that predate the unified cross-platform migration.</p>
<h2 id="inputs-and-outputs">Inputs and outputs</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Input:</strong> a plain text string. Best on short, intent-oriented text.</li>
<li><strong>Output:</strong> a probability distribution over the ~800-emoji vocabulary; take the
top-1 (or top-k). Optimized for <strong>top-1 relevance</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="languages">Languages</h2>
<p>English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Russian, Polish,
Turkish, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified &amp; Traditional), Japanese, Korean, Hindi,
Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Ukrainian, Swedish, Danish, Czech.</p>
<h2 id="limitations">Limitations</h2>
<ul>
<li>Tuned for short, intent-oriented text; long-form text produces noisier suggestions.</li>
<li>Emoji semantics are imprecise; near-ties at the top of the ranking are expected.</li>
<li>Per-language quality varies; lower-resource languages in the set are somewhat weaker.</li>
</ul>
