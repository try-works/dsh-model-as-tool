<h2 id="usage">Usage</h2>
<p><code>Ear</code> names the language of a recording, so an app can pick the right recognizer
before it starts transcribing. It listens to three thirty-second windows rather
than the whole file, which takes about 250 ms.</p>

<h2 id="what-it-hears">What it hears</h2>
<p>A file handed to a transcriber is not speech end to end, so <code>Ear</code> does not
listen to it end to end either. It ranks candidate windows by how much of their
loudness varies at syllable rate - speech rises and falls three to six times a
second and has gaps between words, music sustains, silence does not vary at all</p>
<ul>
<li>and listens to the three most speech-like.</li>
</ul>
<p>That matters more than it sounds. Picking windows by position finds the language
4% of the time on a five-minute recording with speech in a tenth of it. Picking
the loudest windows finds it half the time on a file with a music intro, because
an intro is mixed hotter than the voice after it.</p>
<h2 id="accuracy">Accuracy</h2>
<p>Measured end to end through this SDK, on real uploads:</p>
<table><thead><tr><th></th><th>exact</th><th>confident</th><th>of those, right</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Ordinary recordings</td><td>12/12</td><td>12/12</td><td><strong>12/12</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td>The same, rebuilt as podcasts</td><td>9/10</td><td>8/10</td><td><strong>8/8</strong></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>No confident answer was wrong in either set. The podcast miss is a German
episode read as English under its jingle, and it was reported unsure.</p>
<h2 id="limits">Limits</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Speech mixed under louder music</strong> is read correctly about 60% of the time.
No amount of choosing better windows changes that; the model cannot read it.</li>
<li><strong>Nordic languages</strong> are not distinguished reliably. <code>isReliable</code> is false for
all of them rather than reporting one confidently.</li>
<li><strong>Recordings shorter than thirty seconds</strong> get a single window, so there is
nothing to average and the answer is less certain than the number suggests.</li>
<li>Multilingual recordings are reported as whichever language the chosen windows
contain, not as a mixture.</li>
</ul>
