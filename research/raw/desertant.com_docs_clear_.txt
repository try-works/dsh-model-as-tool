<h2 id="sound">Sound</h2>
<p>Delivers a <strong>rich, present, close-miked podcast sound</strong>.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Denoised.</strong>
HVAC, keyboard clicks, mouse rustle, mic bumps, room hum, laptop
fans, coffee shop background, all pulled down without chewing
consonants.</li>
<li><strong>Dereverbed.</strong>
Untreated bedrooms, offices and hotel rooms come out sounding closer
to a treated studio. The model does not add reverberation of its own.</li>
<li><strong>Warm and present.</strong>
Low-mids brought forward so voice sits comfortably in a mix rather
than sounding thin or distant.</li>
<li><strong>Sibilance-safe.</strong>
No harsh peaks introduced when cleaning up S / T / F consonants.</li>
<li><strong>No pumping or musical-noise artefacts.</strong>
Breaths, plosives and vocal texture stay intact.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="variants">Variants</h2>
<p>Two variants ship. They take the same input, produce the same output format
and cost the same to run, so switching between them is a one-line change in
the SDK. Pick by the sound you want, not by platform: both ship for every
runtime.</p>
<h3 id="clear-studio">clear-studio</h3>
<p>The default. Quiet, studio-like character; silences sit close to true
zero.</p>
<p>Best for solo podcasts, tutorials, voiceover, video demos, screen
recordings, and anything that wants a clean broadcast feel.</p>
<table><thead><tr><th>File</th><th>Purpose</th><th>Size</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><code>clear-studio.<wbr>mlmodelc</code></td><td>Core ML for the Apple Neural Engine (iOS 16 model format)</td><td>9.0 MB</td></tr>

<tr><td><code>clear-studio.<wbr>onnx</code></td><td>Cross-platform ONNX</td><td>24 MB</td></tr></tbody></table>
<h3 id="clear-natural">clear-natural</h3>
<p>Preserves room tone, breath, and lip texture.</p>
<p>For treated podcast studios, intentional voiceover, interviews where
the room is part of the take, and remote guest recordings where
absolute silence would sound wrong.</p>

<h2 id="performance">Performance</h2>
<p>The Core ML variants are optimized for the Apple Neural Engine.
<code>MLComputePlan</code> confirms that all 492 model operations run on ANE.</p>
<p><code>clear-studio</code>, whole SDK pipeline on a 60-second clip, best of three:</p>
<table><thead><tr><th>Device</th><th>Realtime factor</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>iPhone 16 Pro</td><td><strong>302x</strong></td></tr>
<tr><td>MacBook Pro (M5)</td><td><strong>345x</strong></td></tr></tbody></table>
<p>On iPhone 16 Pro, first-ever model loading takes approximately 3.4 seconds
while Core ML compiles the ANE program. Cached launches load in approximately
62 ms; applications should warm the model in the background.</p>
<h2 id="deployment-target">Deployment target</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Core ML model</strong>: iOS/iPadOS 16.0+ model format; ANE placement depends on hardware and OS.</li>
<li><strong>Swift SDK</strong>: iOS 18+, macOS 15+, tvOS 18+, visionOS 2+.</li>
<li><strong>Android</strong>: API 24+ (arm64-v8a, x86_64), via LiteRT.</li>
<li><strong>Other platforms</strong>: the <code>.tflite</code> runs wherever LiteRT does: Linux, Windows, and the browser through LiteRT.js. The ONNX files are kept for runtimes the SDK does not cover.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="what-it-39-s-good-for">What it&#39;s good for</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Meeting recorders.</strong> Zoom, Teams, Meet, Detail exports, single or multi-speaker.</li>
<li><strong>Bluetooth microphones.</strong> AirPods, Sony, headset mics.</li>
<li><strong>Mobile devices.</strong> iPhone and Android built-in microphone recordings, voice notes, field recordings.</li>
<li><strong>Laptop built-in microphones.</strong> MacBook and PC built-in mics.</li>
<li><strong>Untreated rooms.</strong> Bedrooms, hotel rooms, kitchens, coffee shops.</li>
</ul>
<p>Whenever the pitch is <em>messy recording in, clean audio out</em>.</p>
<h2 id="what-it-is-not">What it is not</h2>
<ul>
<li>Not a general-purpose audio denoiser.
Speech is the target; music, effects, and non-vocal signals get pulled down as noise.</li>
<li>Not a source separator.
Overlapping speakers stay overlapping.</li>
<li>Not a voice changer, cloner, or transcription model.</li>
</ul>
