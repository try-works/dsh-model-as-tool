      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Detect language based on 3 words.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>"kann ich das haben" is German. "안녕하세요" is Korean. Three words is the hard case, and three words is what a search box gives you.</p>
          <p>From three words, Tongue scores 0.933 where a 293MB detector scores 0.887. Tongue is 2MB, and works in two stages: some scripts belong to one language only, so Hangul is Korean and Thai is Thai, settled with no model involved. The rest go to the model.</p>
          <p>Tongue needs no tokenizer, vocabulary file, or inference runtime, which is how the whole package fits in 2MB. When the input is too short to call, Tongue reports a tie.</p>
        </div>
      </section>
