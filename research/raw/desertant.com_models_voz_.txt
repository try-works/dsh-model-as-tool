      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Transcribe 10 minutes in 2 seconds.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>Transcribe an audio or video file with Voz and get accurate start and end times for every word. 10 minutes takes 2s on an iPhone, on the device, so nothing is uploaded and nothing is billed per minute.</p>
          <p>On Apple silicon, the model runs on the Neural Engine, so transcribing is lightning fast and consumes less power. On a Mac a back catalog of podcasts, interviews, and lectures runs through in one pass, and nothing is uploaded.</p>
          <p>The timestamps are precise enough to edit on. Word starts land within 83ms of a forced aligner and word ends within 95ms, for precise transcript-based editing. Voz is an Apple Neural Engine optimized version of NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, with accuracy comparable to Whisper large-v3-turbo on most audio, with a download size of only 467MB.</p>
        </div>

      <section class="section rise d5">
        <h2 class="section-title">What the model does</h2>
        <ul class="features">
          <li>Word starts within 83ms and word ends within 95ms of a forced aligner, on average, at 80ms frame resolution.</li>
          <li>10 minutes of audio in 2s on an iPhone 17 Pro, 1.2s on an iPhone 18 Pro, and 2.1s on an iPhone 15 Pro. On an M3 Ultra, 0.8s at 762x realtime. Single short clips run 50-62x, because every clip pays for a full 15s window.</li>
          <li>7.40% word error rate averaged over six Open ASR Leaderboard sets (Whisper large-v3-turbo: 7.00%), 2.83% on half an hour of narration (Whisper: 2.72%), 11.84% on AMI meetings (Whisper: 13.87%).</li>
          <li>The whole graph runs on the Neural Engine with no CPU or GPU fallback; peak memory doesn't grow with the length of the recording.</li>
          <li>25 languages: Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, and Ukrainian.</li>
          <li>467MB on disk, downloaded on demand and cached; the first load after a download takes 20s once while Core ML specializes, then 0.2s. Download during onboarding.</li>
          <li>Mono audio at any sample rate; the SDK resamples and downmixes. Longer audio is cut into 15s windows at pauses and joined on the words neighboring windows agree on.</li>
          <li>Built on NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, released under CC BY 4.0. The weights are unchanged; the conversion, compression, and the Neural Engine runtime are ours.</li>
        </ul>

      <div class="caveats rise d6">
        <p class="model-caveat">The Neural Engine speed is Apple only: on iOS, macOS, tvOS, and visionOS the runtime drives Core ML to keep the whole graph on the Neural Engine. The browser and Node run the same model on WebAssembly and ONNX Runtime, slower than Core ML, and there is no native Android build.</p>
        <p class="model-caveat">Voz doesn't know which of its 25 languages it is hearing, and a language it doesn't cover produces confident nonsense rather than an error, so pair it with Ear. Accuracy varies widely by language, word ends are the harder half of the timestamps, and 467MB is a real download to fetch during onboarding.</p>

        <h2 class="section-title">FAQ</h2>
        <div class="faq">
          <div class="faq-item">
            <h3 class="faq-q">What is Voz?</h3>
            <p class="faq-a">Transcribe 10 minutes of audio in 2s on an iPhone, 14x faster than Whisper, accurate word timestamps.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="faq-item">
            <h3 class="faq-q">Does Voz run on device?</h3>
            <p class="faq-a">Yes. Voz runs on the device, with no server call, so the data stays with the user.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="faq-item">
            <h3 class="faq-q">Which platforms does Voz support?</h3>
            <p class="faq-a">Voz ships as a native on-device SDK for Swift, JavaScript / TypeScript.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="faq-item">
            <h3 class="faq-q">How much does Voz cost?</h3>
            <p class="faq-a">Each model is free up to 100k monthly active devices. Inference is unlimited. <a href="#" role="button" data-mail="Commercial license inquiry">Contact us</a> for custom licenses.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="faq-item">
            <h3 class="faq-q">How accurate or fast is Voz?</h3>
            <p class="faq-a">10 minutes of narration in 2s on an iPhone 17 Pro, 1.2s on an iPhone 18 Pro and 2.1s on an iPhone 15 Pro. On an M3 Ultra, 14x faster than whisper.cpp large-v3-turbo on the same 10-minute podcast, with a 7.40% word error rate over six public English sets where Whisper scores 7.00%.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="faq-item">
            <h3 class="faq-q">Does Voz run outside Apple platforms?</h3>
            <p class="faq-a">Yes, in the browser and in Node. The JavaScript package runs the same model on WebAssembly and ONNX Runtime, so a web app or a server transcribes without Apple hardware, on Windows, Linux, or an Android browser included. On Apple silicon the whole model runs on the Neural Engine, which is where it is fastest. A native Android build is not ready yet.</p>
