      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Suggest a title and description for any text.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>A clip about shooting a podcast on two phones gets the title "Filming a two-person podcast on iPhone", three to eight words, with a one-line description under it. Nobody typed either one.</p>
          <p>The register is deliberately plain: no emoji, no hashtags, no clickbait, and the description says what this passage is, not what its subject is. Fine-tuned on transcript clips, and it holds up on a news paragraph, a product description, or an email.</p>
          <p>Title runs on Apple silicon only, through MLX. Load it once and reuse it; pair it with Clips and every clip leaves with a name.</p>
        </div>
      </section>

      <section class="section section--uc rise d4" id="use-cases">
        <h2 class="section-title">Use cases</h2>
        <div class="uc-figure-grid">
          <figure class="uc-figure">
            <img src="/assets/photos/title-card-816.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1088" height="1360">
            <h3>Suggest a title and description for any text.</h3>
          </figure>
          <div class="uc-figure-col">
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Name every clip</h3><p>Clips finds the moments in a recording; Title names them. A creator app posts each short with a title of three to eight words and a one-line description, written on the phone, with nothing sent anywhere.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Title a note as it's saved</h3><p>A notes app or a voice memo app names the entry from its own text the moment it is saved. The list reads like a list instead of a column of first lines and timestamps.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Describe a recording for the library</h3><p>A podcast or lecture app writes the episode title and the two-sentence description from the transcript on a Mac, ready to edit. The field is never blank until someone gets to it.</p></div>

      <section class="section rise d5">
        <h2 class="section-title">What the model does</h2>
        <ul class="features">
          <li>A title of three to eight words with no final punctuation, and a description of one or two sentences, from one call.</li>
          <li>Plain register on purpose: no emoji, no hashtags, no clickbait. The description identifies this passage rather than its topic.</li>
          <li>Fine-tuned on transcript clips; works on general prose (news paragraphs, product descriptions, emails).</li>
          <li>Names a <code>Clip</code> from Clips directly; one card per clip, in the same order.</li>
          <li>Nothing is downloaded. Ship the model files in a folder with the app and pass that folder to Titles; the other models fetch their weights on first use, this one doesn't.</li>
          <li>MLX rather than Core ML: short generation measured 5.7-8.3x faster on the GPU than on the Neural Engine. A 350M-parameter model quantized to 6-bit, behind the <code>MLX</code> package trait, loaded once and reused.</li>
        </ul>
