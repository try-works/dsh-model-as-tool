      <section class="section section--narrow rise d3" id="overview">
        <h2 class="section-title">Rough sketch. Perfect shape.</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>Draw a rough circle and Shapes snaps it to a clean one. Shapes fits precise vector geometry, snaps to axes, circles, squares and 15-degree rotations, and rejects scribbles that are not shapes.</p>
          <p>On Apple, one call adds Notes-style smart-shape snapping to a PencilKit canvas, and the live preview stays. Apple's own "Snap to Shape" is private API that third-party apps cannot call, so Shapes does the same job in one line.</p>
          <p>The model is small enough to ship inside the app, with no download. In the browser, Shapes runs through LiteRT.js.</p>
        </div>
      </section>
      <section class="section section--perf rise d4" id="performance" aria-label="Performance">
        <h2 class="section-title">Under 10ms per stroke.</h2>
        <p class="section-lede">Under 10ms per stroke, from a 0.2MB model on Apple and 1.3MB on Android and the web.</p>
        <div class="perf-hero no-photo">
          <div class="stat-row">
            <div class="stat"><div class="stat-value">0.2MB</div><div class="stat-label">On-device</div></div>
            <div class="stat"><div class="stat-value">&lt;10ms</div><div class="stat-label">Per recognition</div><div class="stat-detail">on device</div></div>
            <div class="stat"><div class="stat-value">5</div><div class="stat-label">Shape classes</div><div class="stat-detail">rectangle, ellipse, triangle, star, line</div></div>
            <div class="stat"><div class="stat-value">1.3MB</div><div class="stat-label">In the browser</div><div class="stat-detail">fetched once and cached</div></div>
          </div>
        </div>
        <p class="perf-note">Internal measurements. Core ML on Apple, LiteRT.js in the browser, prebuilt native libraries on Node.</p>

      <section class="section section--uc rise d4" id="use-cases">
        <h2 class="section-title">Use cases</h2>
        <div class="uc-figure-grid">
          <figure class="uc-figure">
            <img src="/assets/photos/shapes-card-816.webp" alt="" loading="lazy" width="1088" height="1360">
            <h3>Rough sketch. Perfect shape.</h3>
          </figure>
          <div class="uc-figure-col">
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Notes-style smart shapes</h3><p>Add snap-to-shape to a PencilKit canvas with <code>canvasView.enableShapeSnapping()</code>. Live preview, undo and redo preserved, filling the gap left by Apple's private Snap to Shape API.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Clean up a freehand diagram</h3><p>Turn rough strokes into precise geometry in a diagram or whiteboard tool. Works with tldraw and Excalidraw stroke data.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Cross-platform drawing</h3><p>One recognizer covers iOS, Android, and the web, so you write the snapping code once.</p></div>
            <div class="use-case"><h3>Ships in the app, not as a download</h3><p>On Apple the recognizer is 0.2MB and ships inside the binary, and the Android package bundles it by default. In the browser the SDK fetches 1.3MB once and caches it.</p></div>
